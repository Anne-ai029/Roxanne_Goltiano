Before you
reach out

A few things people usually ask before we work together. If something's missing, just send a message. I don't bite.

01 · What services do you offer? +

End-to-end technical and creative support: Executive Administrative support, Production Workflow, Content Planning, Digital media creation, Brand aligned asset production, Social media content, and Production design.

02 · How long does a typical project take? +

Web page development usually takes 1–4 weeks. Digital asset creation typically ranges 1–5 days. Hit "Let's Connect" and we'll build a timeline together.

03 · What is your pricing model? +

Both fixed-price and hourly arrangements are available. Fixed-price suits well-defined projects; hourly works best for evolving scopes. We'll find the right fit when we talk.

04 · Can I see examples of past work? +

Absolutely, scroll back to Portfolio, or reach out directly and I'll share samples tailored to your specific project type.

05 · How do I get started? +

Click "Let's Connect" or send a direct email. We'll talk through your goals, timeline, and budget. No pressure, just a conversation.

06 · Do you work with international clients? +

Yes! I'm fully remote and have experience working with US-based clients across different time zones. Async communication is no problem at all.
